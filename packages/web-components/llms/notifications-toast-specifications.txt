# cbp-toast

## Purpose

-   A Toast is a way to display messages or notifications, often temporarily, that pop out from the edge of the browser viewport.

## Functional Requirements

-   The Toast component is made of up several parts, which may be customized by the developer:
    -   The Toast shall contain an icon to the left of the message, which is slotted.
    -   The Toast shall have a title, which is slotted.
    -   The toast content is also slotted and may include various content such as the description of an error, a progress bar, etc.
    -   Action buttons may be slotted to dismiss a Toast or perform other application-specific actions.
-   A Toast may be in any of the design system standard colors of: info, success, warning, or danger. The default is "info."
-   A toast may be persistent until dismissed or show for a developer-specified amount of time before disappearing.
-   Multiple toast notifications may be grouped within a container, `cbp-toast-container`, to manage placement (top-left, top-center, top-left, bottom-left, bottom-center, bottom-left), defaulting to top-right.

## Technical Specifications

### User Interactions

-   User interactions via the slotted buttons are application-defined.
-   Toasts should not otherwise contain interactive controls.

### Responsiveness

-   Toasts and the Toast Container are sized to fit on a mobile device screen without scrolling by default.

### Accessibility

-   Toasts using a timer is an accessibility concern, and it is advised to have a log of toasts preserved in the application for reference.

### Additional Notes and Considerations

-   Important notifications should be persisted within the application so they can be revisited in a log format.
